Fasting Timer Privacy Policy

Teseo (hereinafter referred to as the 'Company' or 'Developer') values your personal information and complies with relevant personal information protection laws. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, the purpose of its use, and the protective measures taken when users use the Fasting Timer (hereinafter referred to as the 'App').



1. Items of Personal Information Collected and Method of Collection

This App does not have a membership registration process and does not collect or store sensitive personal information (name, phone number, resident registration number, etc.) on servers to identify users.

However, to provide app features and improve service quality, the following data may be stored inside the device or automatically collected by external services.

Data stored on the device: The target fasting time, weight records, fasting schedule, etc., directly entered by the user are safely stored only inside the user's smartphone device (SQLite DB) and are not transmitted to external servers.

Automatically collected data (3rd party services): App usage records, device information, advertising identifiers (ADID), etc., may be collected through the following third-party services for statistical analysis and advertisement placement.

2. Purpose of Processing Personal Information

The App processes the minimum information for the following purposes:

Service provision: Fasting timer operation, record storage, and statistics provision (processed locally on the device)

Service improvement and statistical analysis: Checking app errors and improving usability using Firebase Analytics

Providing personalized ads: Serving personalized banner ads through Google AdMob

3. Provision and Entrustment of Personal Information to Third Parties

This App does not arbitrarily provide user data to third parties. However, for service operation (statistics and advertisements), the following external SDKs (Software Development Kits) are used, and these companies collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (Personalized Ads): Google Privacy Policy

Firebase Analytics (App Statistics and Analysis): Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Procedure and Method of Destroying Personal Information

Data stored inside the device (fasting records, weight, etc.) is immediately and permanently destroyed when the user deletes (uninstalls) the App from the smartphone or executes 'Clear data' in the device settings.

Since recovery is impossible, caution is required when deleting data.

5. Protection of Children's Personal Information

This App does not intentionally collect personal information from children under the age of 14. If the Developer becomes aware that personal information of a child under 14 has been collected, reasonable steps will be taken to delete such information.

6. Chief Privacy Officer and Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding privacy protection while using the App, please contact us at the following contact information.

Email: hesu6@naver.com

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be modified in accordance with changes in laws or app features. If there are any changes in the contents, they will be notified through announcements within the App or update notices in the app market.
